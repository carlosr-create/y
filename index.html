    What to do now

    1. Paste the prompt into Claude, ChatGPT or any model you trust. It scores each answer and tells you what a partner would push on.
    2. Cut every answer in half. Partners want the number, the name, the date. Not the story.
    3. Fix the cut-offs first. An answer that was interrupted is an answer that started with context instead of the point.
    4. Go again with interruption mode. If your answer survives 8 seconds, it survives the real room.
